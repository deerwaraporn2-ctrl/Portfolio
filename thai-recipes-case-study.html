Thai Recipes Today

Founder & Sole Business Owner (2009–2011)

A culinary platform and lifestyle brand combining authentic Thai recipe content, private cooking workshops in Australia, and an online shop featuring Thai-style home textiles.

The Opportunity

I identified a gap in the Australian market for authentic Thai cooking experiences that combined cultural storytelling, practical education, and accessible digital content.

The Solution

I founded Thai Recipes Today as an integrated platform that brought together recipe publishing, private cooking workshops, and an online store selling Thai-inspired textiles sourced from Thailand.

My Role & Responsibilities

  • Designed and structured the website architecture
  • Created and published original Thai recipe content
  • Published a textbook based on my own recipes
  • Organized and taught private cooking workshops in Australia
  • Sourced and sold Thai-style home textiles via e-commerce
  • Managed bookings, customer communication, and operations

Business Impact

The business operated as a fully independent venture, generating revenue through workshop bookings and product sales while building a loyal community around Thai culinary culture.

Reflection & Modern Rebuild Vision

If I were to rebuild Thai Recipes Today today, I would develop it as a modern, interactive web application using semantic HTML, responsive layouts, component-based architecture, and API-driven content.

Features would include interactive recipe scaling, user dashboards, dynamic filtering, accessibility optimization, and performance-focused deployment workflows.
